## Hatching of the idea
Siyu has long hatched the idea of Sofie, as a birthday gift for Binglun. He wanted to give her a piece to show how special she is. Anyway.. as a congenital condition, Siyu was born was *pectus excavatum*, meaning his chest plate is sunken down into the ribcage and suppress on the heart and lung. Though not lethal, the condition severely affected his cardiopulmonary function, making him unable to even finish a 1000m run. The condition lasted for 19 years until at the age of 19, he decided to go through a surgery to correct this deformity. 

The surgery is a variation on the well-established Nuss procedure. The principle is simple, a steel bar is inserted behind the chest plate to support and lift up the chest plate. The implant is then tied on the ribcage to maintain its stability. The bar needs to be hard, and resistant to corrosion, so is it made of titanium alloy.
